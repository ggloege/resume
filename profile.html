How I Help

From onboarding to adoption to outcomes.

I bring a combination of learning expertise, AI fluency, and a product mindset, developed over 20+ years that began in SaaS customer education: training users in the field on web-based assessment platforms and walking buyers through how the software created value for them.

Underneath it all is a common instinct: treat learners like customers. I build learning as a product, with adoption, engagement, and business impact as the scoreboard. Educated as a jazz composer, I bring that balance of structure and flexibility to architecting learning ecosystems that constantly measure outcomes and evolve accordingly.

Whether you’re scaling a customer education function into a true services offering, or you’re an enterprise adopting AI and managing the messy human side of that transition, I offer clarity, creativity, and rigor for problems that don’t have a playbook yet.

  • Designed the customer onboarding and education experience for Iris: co-designed initial deployments with executive sponsors, coached early cohorts directly, then built onboarding into the product itself as the user base scaled past 1,000.
  • Integrated LLMs across the product and the operation: cut a core design workflow from 5 hours to 5 minutes, generated personalized learning recommendations from user data, and delivered AI micro-coaching to managers inside Slack and email.

  • Advised C-suite leaders on capability strategy; flagship client won the Princess Royal Training Award, with staff development time rising from 1% to 5% of working hours and revenue per hour and profit each improving 10%.
