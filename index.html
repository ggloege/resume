How I Help

Companies bring me in for two reasons:

AI Enablement

Design and deliver learning experiences that get people using AI in ways that impact the bottom line.

AI-Forward L&D

Build and evolve an AI-enabled learning environment that attracts, develops, and retains great talent.

In either case I bring a combination of learning expertise, AI fluency, and a product mindset, developed over 15+ years of leading learning functions in startups, scale-ups, and corporate enterprise.

Underneath it all is a common instinct: I build systems for how people learn and grow. Educated as a jazz composer, I’ve brought that balance of structure and flexibility to creatively architecting learning ecosystems that constantly measure business impact and evolve accordingly.

Whether you’re a scale-up whose growth requires more rigorous systems around core challenges like employee onboarding, manager development, and career pathways, or you’re an enterprise trying to adopt AI and manage the messy human elements of that transition, I bring clarity, creativity, rigor, and a human touch to solving these wicked problems in novel ways.

After sunsetting Iris in 2025, refocused the practice on AI integration advisory.

  • Consulting with services businesses on AI integration to identify high-leverage workflow automations that free human capacity for the client-facing work that drives retention.
  • Built Iris, an AI-enabled talent development platform that visualized role-based employee skill maps to support structured growth conversations, generate custom learning resources, and deliver micro-coaching. LLM integrations reduced core design workflows from 5 hours to 5 minutes.
  • Operated as a trusted advisor to C-suite leaders, diagnosing capability gaps and designing learning ecosystems. Flagship client won the Princess Royal Training Award, with revenue per hour and profit each improving by 10%.
  • Designed and taught Become a Skills-Based Organization on Maven.com (4.4/5.0), an AI-enabled, cohort-based course guiding L&D leaders through building a strategic transformation plan for their own learning function.

Recruited to modernize and reimagine Learning & Organizational Development at a 2,500-person organization, with full accountability for learning strategy, performance consulting, program design, and change management across the enterprise. Led a team of 10 on a $2M budget.

  • Transformed an L&D team of 10 from reactive order-takers into trusted performance consultants by restructuring around client-portfolio ownership and training the team in JTBD analysis, producing stronger stakeholder relationships and faster delivery of business-aligned solutions.
  • Deployed and drove adoption of a modern enterprise LMS across 2,500 employees that tripled monthly active users in 5 months, reduced platform costs 45%, and grew non-required completion rates from 16% to 37%.
  • Designed a 12-week Foundations of Management program that drove a 37% average improvement in skill mastery and correlated with 43% lower turnover on graduate-led teams.
  • Redesigned global onboarding into a 90-day structured experience that increased new-hire satisfaction by 36% while leveraging automation to reduce administrative effort by 75%.
  • Built a company-wide OKR learning ecosystem in approximately one week; achieved 90% system enrollment in the first quarter with only a 7% OKR rejection rate.
  • Spearheaded enterprise change management during a major leadership transition, mapping culture-change levers, building executive messaging frameworks, and mobilizing a cross-company community of change agents without formal authority.
